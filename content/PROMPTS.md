# Copyable prompts for everyday work

Replace the brackets with your details. These are instructions to use in your own task; nothing is executed by reading this page. Start with the everyday version. Add a narrower prompt when it helps.

## Everyday mode

```text
Help me finish [outcome]. Context and material: [details].

For small, clear, reversible work, proceed directly and check it. For substantial or ambiguous work, ask a few targeted questions to settle consequential preferences, then show a brief plan with actions and checks. Wait for my approval to start. If I name websites, ask whether they are priorities or exclusive. For important work, check a representative trial, show it, and wait for my approval before expanding. Fix objective failures within the approved scope yourself. Deliver the result, checks performed, and remaining gaps.
```

## Thorough interview mode

Use this when you cannot yet describe a good result.

```text
Help me define and complete [outcome]. Context: [details]. Start by checking the material and access available to you. Interview me until the consequential preferences and decisions are settled: purpose, audience, priorities, constraints, source choices, output, acceptable actions, and what success would look like.

Group independent questions in adaptive rounds of up to ten; ten is not a total limit. Hold dependent questions until the relevant answers are known. Explain unfamiliar options and their tradeoffs, recommend an answer, then wait for my choice or explicit delegation. Do not treat uncertainty or silence as approval. Investigate discoverable facts yourself. When I name websites, always clarify whether they are priorities or exclusive.

Show a concise recap and a plan covering execution and verification; save the fuller plan if useful. Resolve consequential decisions before requesting approval to start. For important work, check a small representative trial and show its results; wait for my approval before expanding. Repair objective failures within scope automatically. If checks repeatedly fail or access is missing, report the gap instead of claiming success. Deliver the result with evidence and remaining uncertainties.
```

## Ask for a concrete plan

```text
Using our agreed answers, show the intended result, output format, boundaries, actions, and specific checks. Name factual unknowns and how you will investigate them. Flag consequential decisions still needing my answer. Keep the recap short and make any fuller saved plan accessible. Wait for my approval before execution.
```

## Correct one mismatch

```text
Keep [parts that work]. Change [specific mismatch] to [desired result] because [reason]. Ask if this introduces a consequential decision. Update the relevant plan and recheck the affected work. Preserve unrelated content and report what changed.
```

## Set the research scope

```text
Research [question] for [decision], using [named websites]. First ask whether those sites are priorities or exclusive, and explain any proposed additions. Agree on the depth and useful output. Check current factual claims against suitable original sources. For community discussions, examine accessible comments and replies in a defined sample; report coverage gaps. Separate supported facts, reported experiences, preferences, and your inferences. Repeated claims or votes are leads, not verification. Link the evidence and identify what remains unknown.
```

## Check a result

```text
Check this result against the original brief and agreed rules. Use the source material, files, calculations, or cited pages to test whether it works. Report checks actually performed, failures, and gaps you cannot verify. Repair objective failures within scope, then recheck. Ask before changes that alter an agreed decision. Stop repeated unsuccessful attempts and explain what is still unresolved. If this is our trial, show it and wait for my approval before expanding.
```

## Set up recurring work

```text
Help me set up [recurring task]. Clarify the schedule and time zone, inputs, output destination, allowed actions, and what to do when inputs or access are missing. Check whether this setup can run it and how I can inspect results or stop it. Show the plan and wait for approval. For consequential recurring work, complete one run, verify its coverage and accuracy, and show me the result. Wait for approval before scheduling future runs. Include checks that each future result is current and complete. A simple reminder can skip the trial.
```
